Your palettes, hex codes, agency settings, and uploaded images never leave your browser. They are processed locally and stored in your device's localStorage.

3. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics, or third-party ads. We use localStorage strictly for saving your active palettes and license status.
